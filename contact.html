Questions

Common questions

Is there really a 30-day free trial?
Yes, completely free for 30 days. No credit card required to start. You can use every feature of the plan you choose, and if you decide not to continue, you simply stop — no charge, no questions.
Do you take commission on bookings?
Never. Every euro a guest pays for a room goes directly to you. We charge a flat monthly subscription fee only. This is the fundamental difference between MedStay and platforms like Booking.com, which take 15–25% of every reservation.
How long does setup take?
Most hotel owners are fully live within 30 minutes. You add your rooms, upload photos, set your prices, and your booking page is ready. We guide you through every step, and our team is available if you need help.
Can I change plans later?
Yes, anytime. You can upgrade or downgrade between plans. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing month. There are no penalties or fees for switching.
What languages does MedStay support?
The dashboard is available in English and French. Guest-facing messages are automatically sent in Arabic, French, Italian, German, or English depending on the guest's preference — available on Professional and Business plans.
What if I need help getting started?
We are available by email and WhatsApp to help you set up your hotel profile and answer any questions. Business plan customers get a dedicated onboarding session by video call.
