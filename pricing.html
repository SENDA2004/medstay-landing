Common questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes — every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. No credit card required. You can cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged anything.
Does MedStay take commission on bookings?
Never. Unlike Booking.com which takes 15–25% per booking, MedStay charges a flat monthly fee. Every euro your guests pay goes directly to you.
Can I switch plans later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing month.
What languages does the platform support?
The MedStay dashboard is available in Italian, French, Arabic, and English. Guest communications can be automatically sent in any of these plus German.
How long does setup take?
Most hotels are fully live within 30 minutes. You add your rooms, set your pricing, upload photos, and your booking page is ready. No technical skills needed.
